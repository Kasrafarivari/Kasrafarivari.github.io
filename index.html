About

I'm a Senior Product Manager at Yahoo, focused on consumer product growth and the intersection of mobile experiences and emerging AI capabilities. I joined Yahoo in 2025 after three years at Product Madness, the Aristocrat-owned mobile games studio, where I led the New User Experience and Web Shop across a portfolio of top-grossing titles. Earlier, I was a Product Manager at Big Fish Games (2021–2022).

I hold an MBA from Georgetown University, I'm based in Maryland, and I'm an avid video gamer. I write about product management and AI from a practitioner's perspective — what's actually working in my workflow, what isn't, and the parts of the job that have quietly changed in the last two years.
